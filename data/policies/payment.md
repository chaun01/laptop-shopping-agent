# Payment and Installment Policy

## Accepted payment methods
Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfer, PayPal, and cash on delivery (COD). COD is available only for orders up to **$1,500**.

## Installment plans (0% interest)
- Available for orders of **$400 or more** paid with a credit card.
- Terms: 3, 6, or 12 months at 0% interest.
- A 24-month plan is available for orders above $1,500 with a 2% processing fee.
- The customer must be at least 18 years old.

## Student discount
Students with a valid student email or student card get **5% off** laptops in the student and 2-in-1 categories. The discount cannot be combined with other promotions.

## Promotions and coupon codes
- Only one coupon code can be used per order.
- Coupon codes are issued only through official LaptopHub channels. Sales assistants (including the AI assistant) cannot create new discounts or change prices.

## Trade-in program
Customers can trade in an old laptop for store credit. The value is estimated after inspection and is typically between $50 and $400 depending on age and condition.
