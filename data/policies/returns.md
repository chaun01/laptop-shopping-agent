# Return and Refund Policy

## Return window
You may return a laptop within **15 days** of delivery for a full refund, provided that:
- The laptop is in like-new condition with no physical damage.
- All accessories, the charger, and the original box are included.
- The device is not locked to a personal account (remove your Apple ID / Microsoft account).

## Restocking fee
- Unopened laptops: no fee.
- Opened laptops in like-new condition: **10% restocking fee**.
- Gaming laptops that have been opened: **15% restocking fee**.

## Defective on arrival (DOA)
If the laptop is defective within the first **7 days**, we offer a free exchange or a full refund with no restocking fee.

## Non-returnable items
- Laptops with physical or liquid damage caused by the customer.
- Laptops purchased in clearance sales marked "final sale".

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5-7 business days** after we receive and inspect the laptop. Refunds above $1,000 require approval from a store manager.

## Price adjustment
If the price of a laptop you bought drops within **7 days** of purchase, we refund the difference once per order.
