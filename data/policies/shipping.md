# Shipping Policy

## Delivery options
| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | Free for orders above $500, otherwise $15 |
| Express | 1-2 business days | $25 |
| Same-day (selected cities) | Same day if ordered before 12:00 | $40 |

## Out-of-stock items
Laptops with zero stock cannot be ordered. Customers can request a back-in-stock notification. Restocks usually take **10-15 days**.

## Order changes
The shipping address can be changed until the order status becomes "shipped". Orders can be cancelled free of charge before shipping.

## Inspection on delivery
Customers may open the package and inspect the laptop in front of the courier. Report missing items or visible damage within **24 hours** of delivery.
