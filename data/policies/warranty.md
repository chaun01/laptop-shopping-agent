# Warranty Policy

## Standard warranty
All laptops sold by LaptopHub include a **12-month manufacturer warranty** from the delivery date. Apple laptops include Apple's standard 12-month limited warranty. Business-series laptops (Latitude X, EliteNote, ThinkEdge, TravelPro) include a **36-month** on-site warranty.

## What is covered
- Manufacturing defects in hardware: motherboard, screen, keyboard, ports, battery.
- Battery is covered if capacity falls below 80% within the first 12 months.
- Dead pixels: 3 or more dead pixels on the display qualify for a screen replacement.

## What is not covered
- Physical damage (drops, cracks), liquid damage, and damage from unauthorized repairs.
- Software issues, viruses, and data loss. Always back up your data before sending a device for repair.
- Cosmetic wear such as scratches.

## Extended warranty (LaptopHub Care+)
Customers can buy **Care+** within 30 days of purchase:
- Care+ 2 years: 8% of the laptop price. Extends coverage to 24 months total.
- Care+ 3 years with accidental damage: 15% of the laptop price. Covers 1 accidental damage claim per year with a $49 service fee.

## How to claim
Open a support ticket with your order ID and a description of the problem. Repairs take **7-14 business days**. If a repair is not possible, we replace the laptop with the same or an equivalent model.
